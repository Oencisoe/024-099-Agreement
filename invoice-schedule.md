## Terms & structure

- **Work week:** Sunday through Saturday, starting **Sunday, Feb 1, 2026**.
- **Invoice due to FMS:** the **Monday after** the week closes (the Monday following Saturday).
- **Net 30:** payment is due **30 days from that Monday**.
- The screenshots show the **bank deposit date** (the "Actual paid" column). It is matched to
  the week whose Net‑30 due date is closest; "Variance" is paid date − Net‑30 due.
- **Inclusion:** a payment is listed if it references **Coastal/CGSS** OR is **≈ $4,200**
  (the recurring invoice amount) even when the memo names a different payee.
