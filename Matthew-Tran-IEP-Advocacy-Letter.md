## 1. Purpose of This Letter

We are writing to formally request an **IEP team meeting** to review Matthew's current program and to discuss our concern that **Matthew is not making meaningful progress on his IEP goals** and requires **more individualized instruction** to receive a free appropriate public education (FAPE).

Specifically, we are requesting that the team:

1. Review Matthew's present levels of performance and his progress (or lack of progress) on each current goal, using objective data.
2. Increase the amount of **specialized, individualized (1:1 and small-group) instruction** Matthew receives, particularly in **reading and math**.
3. Revise instructional **methodology** so that it is matched to how Matthew actually learns and motivated by his documented strengths and interests.
4. Consider whether additional **assessments** are needed (academic, assistive technology / AAC, speech-language, and functional behavior) to inform an appropriately individualized program.

We are requesting this meeting in writing so that the district can schedule it within the timeline required by law. Please confirm a date in writing.

---

## 2. Why We Are Concerned — Lack of Progress

Matthew is described across his service records as **making limited progress on his IEP goals** and needing **additional academic support beyond what is currently offered**. Our direct observations and outside-provider input are consistent with this:

- Matthew's **reading and math skills remain below grade level**, and the gap between Matthew and his peers is not closing under his current program.
- Matthew needs help to **catch up with peers in both education and socialization**.
- Matthew has **very specific, narrow interests** and **struggles in academic and social settings** when instruction is not individualized to him.
- We have separately had to seek **private tutoring (1 hour per day, 5 days per week)** to supplement school instruction — which tells us the current level of individualized instruction at school is not sufficient.

> **Legal basis:** Under the IDEA, when a child is **not making expected progress** toward annual goals, the IEP team **must revise the IEP** to address that lack of progress (34 C.F.R. § 300.324(b)(1)(ii)(A)). An IEP must be **"reasonably calculated to enable a child to make progress appropriate in light of the child's circumstances"** (*Endrew F. v. Douglas County School District*, 137 S. Ct. 988 (2017)). A program that produces minimal or no progress is not sufficient.

## 4. Specific Requests for Increased Individualized Instruction

We are requesting the IEP team **discuss and document** the following. (We understand the team makes the final decisions collaboratively; these are our proposals as equal IEP team members.)

### A. More individualized (1:1 / small-group) academic instruction
- Increase the amount of **specialized academic instruction** delivered **1:1 or in a small group**, especially in **reading and math**, so instruction is targeted to Matthew's actual present levels rather than to the class as a whole.
- Provide **explicit, systematic, individualized instruction** with frequent guided practice, immediate feedback, and data collection on each goal.
- Consider **additional support time / instructional aide support** during academic blocks so Matthew receives consistent individualized attention and prompting.

### B. Strengths-based, interest-driven methodology
- Embed Matthew's **technology and content-creation interests** into reading and math instruction (e.g., using typing/keyboarding, screen-based tasks, video-modeling, and his interest in drawing/animation to teach and generalize academic skills).
- Use **video modeling and visual supports**, since Matthew demonstrably learns by watching and copying tutorials.
- Build instruction around **clear, predictable routines and visual schedules**, consistent with how Matthew learns best.

### C. Assistive technology and communication
- Conduct (or review) an **Assistive Technology assessment**, including evaluation for an **AAC / iPad-based communication device**, to support Matthew's ability to communicate in full sentences and to access academic instruction.
- Identify the **assistive technology and software** Matthew needs to participate in instruction in his areas of strength.

### D. Measurable, individualized goals with progress monitoring
- Rewrite or add **measurable annual goals** in reading, math, and communication that reflect Matthew's **individual present levels** and include **baselines, objective criteria, and a schedule for reporting progress** to us.
- Establish a clear plan for **how progress will be measured and how often it will be reported**, so that lack of progress is caught and addressed promptly.

### E. Behavior and safety support that protects instructional access
- Review the **Behavior Intervention Plan / behavior supports**, including a current or updated **Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA)**, addressing mouthing, head-banging, self-stimulatory behavior, and **elopement** (a documented safety risk, including during transitions). Behavior that removes Matthew from instruction or endangers him must be addressed for him to access individualized instruction.
- Coordinate with Matthew's outside **ABA provider** (with our consent) so school and home strategies are consistent.

### F. Related services
- Evaluate the need for, and coordinate, **speech-language** and **occupational therapy** supports in the school setting, and ensure they are integrated with his individualized academic program.
